**Lesson 15. Limits of Sequences**

**Terminology:**

Limit of a sequence

Limit operations

Infinite geometric series

Sum of an infinite geometric series

**Knowledge and skills:**

Recognize the concept of the limit of a sequence.

Explain some basic limits.

Apply limit operations to find the limits of simple sequences.

Calculate the sum of an infinite geometric series and apply this result to solve hypothetical or practical real-world situations.

**1. Finite Limits of Sequences**

**a) Recognizing Sequences with a Limit of 0**

We say that a sequence $(u_n)$ has a limit of $0$ as $n$ tends to positive infinity if $|u_n|$ can be made smaller than any arbitrarily small positive number from a certain term onwards, denoted as $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = 0$ or $u_n \to 0$ as $n \to +\infty$.

**Note:** From the definition of a sequence having a limit of $0$, we obtain the following results:

• $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} \frac{1}{n^k} = 0$ where $k$ is a positive integer;

• $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} q^n = 0$ if $|q| < 1$.

• If $|u_n| \le v_n$ for all $n \ge 1$ and $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} v_n = 0$, then $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = 0$.

**b) Recognizing Sequences with Finite Limits**

We say that a sequence $(u_n)$ has a limit equal to the real number $a$ as $n$ tends to positive infinity if $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} (u_n - a) = 0$, denoted as $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = a$ or $u_n \to a$ as $n \to +\infty$.

**Note:** If $u_n = c$ ($c$ is a constant), then $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = c$.

We have the equivalent relation: $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = a \Leftrightarrow \lim\_{n\to+\infty} (u_n - a) = 0$.

**2. Theorems on Finite Limits of Sequences**

**a) Rules for Limit Calculations**

In general, we have the following limit calculation rules:

a) If $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = a$ and $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} v_n = b$, then:

• $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} (u_n + v_n) = a + b$;

• $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} (u_n - v_n) = a - b$;

• $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} (u_n \cdot v_n) = a \cdot b$;

• $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} \frac{u_n}{v_n} = \frac{a}{b}$ (if $b \neq 0$).

b) If $u_n \ge 0$ for all $n$ and $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = a$, then $a \ge 0$ and $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} \sqrt{u_n} = \sqrt{a}$.

**Method:** To find the limit of a fractional sequence, divide both the numerator and the denominator by the highest power of $n$, then apply the limit calculation rules.

**3. Sum of an Infinite Geometric Series**

**a) Concept of an Infinite Geometric Series**

An infinite geometric sequence $(u_n)$ with common ratio $q$ satisfying $|q| < 1$ is called an infinite geometric series (or infinite decreasing geometric progression).

**b) Sum Formula**

Given an infinite geometric series $(u_n)$ with common ratio $q$ ($|q| < 1$). The sum of the infinite geometric series is determined by the formula:

$$S = u_1 + u_2 + \dots + u_n + \dots = \frac{u_1}{1 - q}$$

**4. Infinite Limits of Sequences**

**a) Definition of Infinite Limits**

• A sequence $(u_n)$ is said to have a limit of $+\infty$ as $n \to +\infty$ if $u_n$ can be made greater than any arbitrary positive number from a certain term onwards, denoted as $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = +\infty$ or $u_n \to +\infty$ as $n \to +\infty$.

• A sequence $(u_n)$ is said to have a limit of $-\infty$ as $n \to +\infty$ if $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} (-u_n) = +\infty$, denoted as $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = -\infty$ or $u_n \to -\infty$ as $n \to +\infty$.

**b) Commonly Used Basic Limits**

• $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} n^k = +\infty$ where $k$ is a positive integer;

• $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} q^n = +\infty$ where $q > 1$.

**c) Rules for Infinite Limits**

Regarding infinite limits of sequences, we have the following rules:

• If $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = a$ and $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} v_n = \pm\infty$, then $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} \frac{u_n}{v_n} = 0$.

• If $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = a > 0$, $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} v_n = 0$, and $v_n > 0$ for all $n$, then $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} \frac{u_n}{v_n} = +\infty$.

• If $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} u_n = +\infty$ and $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} v_n = a > 0$, then $\lim\_{n\to+\infty} (u_n \cdot v_n) = +\infty$.
